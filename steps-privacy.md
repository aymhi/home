# Steps: Routine Timer — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 12, 2026
**Developer:** Aymhi Consulting Corp.

This policy explains how Steps: Routine Timer ("Steps", "the app") handles your information on iPhone and Apple Watch. The short version: **Steps does not collect any data about you.** We have no account system, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

## Information we collect

None. Aymhi Consulting Corp. does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal information or usage data from the app.

## Information stored by the app

The app stores the following so it can work. All of it stays on your devices or in your own iCloud account, and none of it is accessible to us:

- **Your categories, routines and steps** — names, instructions, durations, rest times, colours, icons, favourites and their order.
- **Your app settings** — sound, haptics, countdown cues, keep-screen-awake and Live Activity preferences. These are stored on the device only.

## iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Steps syncs your routines between your iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's CloudKit **private database**. That data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer has no access to your private iCloud database and cannot see, read or recover its contents.

If you are not signed in to iCloud, your routines are stored on the device only.

## Notifications and Live Activities

Steps asks for permission to send notifications so it can alert you when a step finishes while the app is in the background. These are **local notifications** scheduled on your device; no notification content is sent to us or through our servers (we have none). The Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island is also generated on your device.

You can turn notifications off at any time in **Settings › Notifications › Steps**, and Live Activities off in the app's Settings screen.

## Permissions the app does not use

Steps does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or Apple Health (HealthKit) data, and it does not track you across apps or websites. It does not use your device's advertising identifier.

## Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings (**Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › Share With App Developers**), Apple may provide us with anonymised crash reports and diagnostics. This is controlled by Apple and by you; the app itself contains no crash-reporting or analytics code.

## Deleting your data

- **Individual routines or categories:** delete them in the app. With iCloud on, the deletion syncs to your other devices.
- **Everything on a device:** delete the app.
- **Everything in iCloud:** on your iPhone, go to **Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage**, select Steps, and delete its data.

Because we never hold a copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## Children's privacy

Steps is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Steps changes how data is handled — for example, by adding advertising — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
