# Steps: Routine Timer — Support

Steps turns anything you do in a fixed order — a workout, a recipe, a morning routine — into a timer that runs itself, on iPhone and Apple Watch.

## Contact us

Email **support@aymhi.ca** with your question or problem. We aim to reply within **2 business days**.

To help us help you, please include:

- Your iPhone model and iOS version (and Apple Watch model and watchOS version, if relevant)
- The Steps version — shown in the app under **Settings › Version**
- What you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead
- A screenshot, if it helps

## Requirements

- iPhone running **iOS 18** or later
- Apple Watch (optional) running **watchOS 11** or later, paired with an iPhone that has Steps installed
- An iCloud account is optional; it is needed only to sync routines to Apple Watch and between devices

## Frequently asked questions

### I'm not getting alerts when a step finishes while my phone is locked.

Steps uses notifications to alert you at each step boundary when the app is in the background. Check **Settings › Notifications › Steps** on your iPhone and make sure **Allow Notifications** is on. The app's own Settings screen shows the current permission status and has a shortcut to fix it. Also check that a Focus mode or Silent mode isn't muting alerts.

### I don't see the countdown on my Lock Screen or Dynamic Island.

Make sure **Live Activity** is turned on in the app's Settings screen, and that Live Activities are allowed for Steps in **Settings › Steps** on your iPhone.

### The timer seems to have stopped while the app was in the background.

The countdown is tied to the clock, not to the app being open, so it keeps its place when you lock your phone or switch apps. When you return to Steps it will be on the correct step with the correct time remaining. If notifications are off, you just won't be alerted as each step finishes.

### My Apple Watch shows no routines.

Routines are created on iPhone and reach the watch through iCloud. Check that:

1. You're signed in to the **same iCloud account** on both devices.
2. iCloud is enabled for Steps on your iPhone (**Settings › [your name] › iCloud**). In Steps, **Settings › Sync** should say **iCloud**.
3. You've opened Steps on your iPhone at least once, and both devices are connected to the internet.

Sync can take a few minutes, especially the first time.

### My routines aren't syncing between devices.

Confirm both devices use the same iCloud account and have iCloud Drive and an internet connection. If your iCloud storage is full, sync will pause — free up space or upgrade your plan. Opening Steps on each device prompts it to check for changes.

### A step should wait for me instead of running on a timer.

Set the step's duration to zero and it will wait for you to tap to continue. You can also turn off auto-advance so every step waits — handy for cooking.

### How do I change the rest time for one step?

Open the step in the editor and turn on the custom rest option for that step. It overrides the routine's default rest.

### Can I turn off the sounds or vibrations?

Yes. In the app's **Settings** screen you can toggle **Sound**, **Haptics**, the **last-three-seconds countdown**, and **Keep screen awake** independently.

### Do I need an account?

No. Steps has no sign-up and no account. Your routines live on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account.

### How do I delete my data?

Delete routines or categories in the app, or delete the app to remove everything from a device. To remove synced data from iCloud, go to **Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage**, select Steps, and delete its data. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md) for details.

### Which languages does Steps support?

English, French, German, Japanese, Spanish, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. The app follows your device's language setting.
